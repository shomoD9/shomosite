# Shomodip.com System Design

## Purpose

Shomodip.com is the permanent archive for Shomodip De's writing, products, skills, and product documentation.

The archive receives content directly. The Master Obsidian vault is no longer a source, synchronization target, or publishing dependency.

## Writing Intake

Supplying a writing link means the piece belongs in the archive unless Shomodip explicitly says otherwise.

An imported piece receives:

- a permanent `/prose/<slug>/` route;
- a site-hosted HTML rendition;
- original-source attribution and URL;
- original publication date and site-added date;
- one primary topic and optional tags;
- a chosen design family;
- optional manually selected related items;
- automatically calculated backlinks.

Sources remain metadata. The site does not create source-publication landing pages.

## Product And Skill Intake

Products and skills share one catalog. Each entry receives a root overview, a lifecycle status, related Prose, and a nested documentation tree.

Skills are cataloged as products with `itemType: skill`.

## Archive Browsing

Prose is browsable by primary topic, complete chronology, search, related items, and backlinks.

Product is browsable as one catalog with type and lifecycle filters.

The homepage remains an editorial front door with selected work, recent additions, and direct paths to Prose, Product, About, and Design.

## Stable Capabilities

The site retains global search, responsive pages, direct-link attachments, and scrollable hover-preview windows. Visual redesign and HTML-generation tooling can evolve without changing the public IA.
