I am Shomodip De, a product manager and writer interested in AI, knowledge systems, product design, psychology, productivity, philosophy, and culture.

Shomodip.com is the permanent home for work I first publish elsewhere and for documentation about the products and skills I build. When I supply a writing link, the piece receives a readable page here while retaining its original source and publication date.

## Explore

- **Prose** organizes writing by five broad topics and also provides a complete chronological archive.
- **Product** contains products, skills, lifecycle status, and nested documentation.
- **Design** explains the page families and reading conventions shared across the site.

Search is available from every page. Internal links can be inspected in scrollable preview windows without leaving the current page.
